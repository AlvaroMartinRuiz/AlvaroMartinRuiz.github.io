This study is the thesis I presented for the Bachelor's in Data Science and Engineering, but in reality it is a compendium of all the work I have been developing over the last year as a Research Assistant in the Department of Signal Theory and Communications at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid, under the supervision of Antonio Artés and in collaboration with the company Evidence-Based Behavior.

It has been an amazing experience, where I have learnt what the day-to-day of a researcher looks like and had the opportunity to work with incredible minds and people.

The research investigates whether data collected from the smartphones of at-risk patients can provide a useful signal to help prevent suicide attempts. It has not been an easy project, given the small size of the dataset and the irregularity of the data collected. On top of that, a suicide-risk event is not a common episode in a patient's life, which brings the challenge of extremely imbalanced classes. Nevertheless, the greater the problem, the more you learn.
