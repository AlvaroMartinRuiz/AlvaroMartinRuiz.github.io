## Overview

Job seekers rarely get a precise answer to a simple question: *what exactly am I missing for the role I want?* Skill Gap Analysis turns that into a data problem — it extracts the skills a candidate already has, compares them against the requirements of a target role, and surfaces the concrete gaps to close.

## How it works

- **Skill extraction (NLP)** — parses CVs and job descriptions to pull out a normalised set of skills.
- **Graph-based analysis** — represents skills and roles as a graph, so related and prerequisite skills are taken into account rather than treating each skill in isolation.
- **Gap report** — the difference between the two skill sets becomes a ranked list of what to learn next.
